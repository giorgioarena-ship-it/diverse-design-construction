About us

A family-run crew built on repeat customers

Diverse Design Construction Inc. started in 2000 as a two-person fencing crew and has grown into a small, dependable team that still shows up to every job site in person.

Our story

Background

We got our start building a single backyard fence for a neighbor, and the referrals never really stopped. Today we work with homeowners, property managers, and the occasional small farm who want a fence or deck that's built right the first time — without the runaround of a big-box contractor.

Our Mission

To build fences and decks that hold up to real weather and real use, backed by clear pricing and a crew that actually answers the phone when something needs a follow-up.

What we believe

Our Values

  • Built to last, not just to look good on day one. We use materials and fasteners rated for the job, not the cheapest option.
  • Straight pricing. The quote we give you up front is the number you pay, barring changes you approve.
  • We clean up after ourselves. Every job site gets cleared of scrap and debris before we call it done.
  • Honest timelines. We'd rather tell you two weeks and deliver in ten days than the other way around.

How we work

Our Process

  1. Free site estimate — we measure your yard and talk through what you want.
  2. Written quote — a clear, itemized estimate you can review before committing.
  3. Material selection — pick your wood, composite, or fence style and finish.
  4. Build — our crew completes the project on the agreed schedule.
  5. Walk-through — we inspect the finished work with you before leaving.
